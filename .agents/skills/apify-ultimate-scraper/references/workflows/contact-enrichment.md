# Contact enrichment workflows

## Website contact extraction from a URL list
1. `vdrmota/contact-info-scraper` or `compass/contact-details-scraper-standby`
2. `apify/social-media-leads-analyzer`

## LinkedIn warm lead identification from post comments
1. `harvestapi/linkedin-post-comments`
2. `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-scraper`

## Real-time lead enrichment on form submission
1. `apify/website-content-crawler`
2. `harvestapi/linkedin-company` (optional)
3. AI ICP scoring node
