# Company research workflows

## Company intelligence profiling for sales or ABM
1. `apify/website-content-crawler`
2. `harvestapi/linkedin-company`
3. AI signal extraction
4. Store to Supabase/Airtable/HubSpot

## Startup scouting from Product Hunt
1. `apify/web-scraper` on Product Hunt
2. `apify/website-content-crawler`
3. `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-search`
4. AI ICP scoring

## Sales meeting prep from LinkedIn and news
1. Google Calendar trigger
2. `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-scraper` + `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-posts`
3. `harvestapi/linkedin-company`
4. `apify/google-search-scraper`
5. AI brief synthesis
6. Deliver via Gmail/WhatsApp
